My wonderful wife is studying elementary pedagogy.
One of the assignments for English teaching class was to introduce a letter from the alphabet with some poetry/rhyme and movement to the kids. 
I was recently watching some [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tG9OKz0jsUY) about bees and I was fascinated with their puns. 
That inspired me to write this rhyme for her. 
My honey come up with some dance to it. 
I directed and filmed it (which I won't show here). 
And just like, the assignment was done. 

The rhyme is not much, but its my first English, also all words starting with letter B.
I had so much joy writing it.

# B(ee) 
Bee's buzzing
Between bushes
Busy brushing
Blissful bouquets
