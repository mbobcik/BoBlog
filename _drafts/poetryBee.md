My wonderful wife is studying elementary pedagogy.
One of the assignments for English teaching class was to introduce a letter from the alphabet with some poetry/rhyme and movement. 
I was recently watching some [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tG9OKz0jsUY) about bees and I was fascinated with their puns. 
So that inspired me to write this rhyme for her. 
My honey come up with some dance to it. 
I directed and filmed it (which I won't show here). 
And just like, the assignment is done. 

# B(ee) 
Bee's buzzing
Between bushes
Busy brushing
Blissful bouquets
